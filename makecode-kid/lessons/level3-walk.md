# Level 3: walk
Goal: make the whole robot walk.

1. `on start`: **start walker**.
2. `on button A`: **walk forward for 5 seconds**.
3. `on button B`: **walk turn left for 3 seconds**.
4. Change the numbers: **step size**, **foot lift**, **seconds for 4 steps**, **lean over standing legs**. Make a race: who gets the fastest robot that does not fall over?
5. Open the Walker Designer, change leg length or the leg direction, and use the same walking numbers it shows on the Finish page.

If the robot tips over when a leg lifts, raise **lean** or lower **foot lift**.
