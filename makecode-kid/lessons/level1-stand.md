# Level 1: stand up and strike a pose
Goal: stand, then make a pose. Needs: legs attached, robot standing on a table.

1. `on start`: **start walker**. The robot should stand with all joints in the middle.
2. `on button A`: **set** front right **knee** to 30 degrees. The foot lifts. Press **stand** on button B to put it down.
3. Make a pose: wave one leg, crouch (all knees to -20), or lean.
4. Which angles feel too far? The hips stop at 45 degrees and the knees at -30 / 60 to protect the printed parts.
