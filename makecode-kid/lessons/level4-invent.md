# Level 4: invent
Pick one:
- **Remote control:** two micro:bits with the `radio` blocks. Tilt the remote to walk forward, back, left, right.
- **Dance:** `walk turn left for 2 seconds`, `stand`, wave a front leg, repeat with music.
- **Obstacle stopper:** ultrasonic sensor, stop and turn when something is near.
- **Faces:** show a happy face when walking and a sleepy face when standing.
Write down your idea first, then build it in small steps.
