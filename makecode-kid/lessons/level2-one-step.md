# Level 2: take one step with one leg
Goal: lift, swing forward, put down, swing back. The body will not move much. That is the idea of walking.

1. **set** front left knee to 25 (lift). **pause** 300 ms.
2. **set** front left hip to 20 (foot forward). **pause** 300.
3. **set** front left knee to 0 (put down). **pause** 300.
4. **set** front left hip to 0 (sweep back). **pause** 300.
5. Put it in a **repeat 4 times** loop.

Think: why only one leg at a time? Try lifting two legs together and see what happens.
